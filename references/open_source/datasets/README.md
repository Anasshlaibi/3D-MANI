# Datasets Intake & Review (`/references/open_source/datasets`)

## Audited Datasets

### 1. Fusion 360 Gallery Dataset
- **Repository:** https://github.com/AutodeskAILab/Fusion360GalleryDataset
- **Owner:** Autodesk AI Lab
- **Content:** CAD reconstruction sequences, feature segmentation, assembly joints, sketch constraints.
- **License Warning:** Non-commercial / research license constraints.
- **Strict Policy:** Must **NOT** enter commercial training without formal legal approval.
- **Status in Registry:** `LICENSE_STATUS = PENDING_LEGAL_REVIEW / RESEARCH_ONLY`

### 2. CADGenBench
- **Repository:** https://github.com/huggingface/cadgenbench
- **Owner:** Hugging Face / Community
- **License:** Apache-2.0
- **Role:** Benchmark and evaluation fixture suite for measuring AI CAD generation fidelity against ground-truth STEP and B-Rep structures.
- **Status in Registry:** `LICENSE_STATUS = APPROVED` (Evaluation benchmark only)

### 3. ABC Dataset
- **Owner:** NYU Courant Institute
- **License:** MIT / Academic Collection
- **Role:** Large collection of explicit B-Rep and polygonal models for geometric benchmark testing.
- **Status in Registry:** `LICENSE_STATUS = APPROVED` (Benchmarking)
